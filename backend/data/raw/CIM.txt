Acme Robotics — Confidential Information Memorandum (excerpt)

Company: Acme Robotics, Inc.
Headquarters: Austin, TX
Sector: Industrial automation / warehouse robotics

Business overview
Acme Robotics designs and sells autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for mid-market
warehouses. The company has 420 employees as of December 31, 2024.

Key financial metrics (management view)
Revenue FY2024: $52.9M
Revenue FY2023: $38.1M
Gross margin FY2024: 58%
EBITDA FY2024: $6.2M
ARR exiting FY2024: $41.0M
Customers: 186 active warehouse sites

Growth commentary
Management attributes FY2024 revenue growth to expansion with existing 3PL
customers and two new enterprise logos signed in Q3 FY2024.
